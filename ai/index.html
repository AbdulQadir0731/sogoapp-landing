AI videos

Make AI videos in SogoApp with Seedance

SogoApp uses your own Seedance account by BytePlus to make AI videos. You create the account and an API key once, paste the key into SogoApp, and you are ready to go.

Your API key stays on your phone. SogoApp never sees or stores it. The app sends your request straight from your phone to BytePlus.

  1. 1

    Create your BytePlus account

    Seedance is made by BytePlus. Sign up for a free BytePlus account with your email. The BytePlus console is in English.

    Create my BytePlus accountOpens the BytePlus sign-up page
    Screenshot coming soon
  2. 2

    Turn on Seedance in ModelArk

    1. Sign in to the BytePlus console.
    2. Open ModelArk.
    3. Find a Seedance video model and activate it.

    BytePlus charges for each video it generates, according to its own pricing. You can see the pricing and your usage in the BytePlus console.

    Screenshot coming soon
  3. 3

    Create an API key

    1. In ModelArk, open API keys.
    2. Tap Create.
    3. Copy the new key. Treat it like a password and do not share it with anyone.
    Screenshot coming soon
  4. 4

    Paste the key into SogoApp

    1. Open SogoApp and go to Reels.
    2. Open the camera and choose AI video.
    3. Tap I already have a key.
    4. Paste your key. Done — you can make your first AI video.

Stuck?

My key didn't work

Copy the key again from ModelArk → API keys and paste it again — make sure nothing is missing at the start or end and there are no extra spaces.

Also check that a Seedance video model is activated in ModelArk (step 2). A key works only for models that are turned on.

BytePlus says my region is not supported

BytePlus does not offer its services in every country or region. If sign-up or Seedance is not available where you are, SogoApp cannot change that — check BytePlus's own list of supported regions.

It says I'm out of credit

Generation is paid to BytePlus, not to SogoApp. Top up your balance in the BytePlus console, then try again in SogoApp.

What happens to my key, prompt and photos?

Your key is stored only on your phone. SogoApp never sees or stores it.

Your prompt and any photo you choose are sent to BytePlus to make the video, and are handled under BytePlus's terms and privacy policy. You can remove the key from the app at any time, and you can delete or replace the key in the BytePlus console.

AI-generated label

Videos made this way are labelled “AI-generated” in SogoApp, so people always know.

AI videos follow the same rules as everything else you share. Please read our Community Guidelines.
