Getting Started

Create your account and get set up.

How do I create a Sogo account?

Signing up takes about a minute — all you need is your phone number.

  1. Open Sogo and tap Get Started on the welcome screen.
  2. Your country is detected automatically — tap the flag to change it if needed.
  3. Enter your phone number and tap Get OTP.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code we text you to verify your number.
  5. Add your name (and an optional photo) to finish your profile.
Tip

If country detection doesn't kick in, just tap the flag and pick your country manually.

How do view-once photos and videos work?

View-once media lets you send a photo or video that can be opened only one time. After it's viewed it's gone — it isn't saved to the chat or the gallery.

  1. Attach a photo or video in a chat.
  2. Turn on View once before sending.
  3. The other person can open it a single time; once they close it, it can't be reopened.
Important

Screenshots and screen recording are blocked while a view-once photo or video is open. View-once media also expires automatically if it's never opened.

How do I receive a call?

Incoming calls ring with your phone's native call screen — even when Sogo is closed.

  • Accept to join the call.
  • Decline to dismiss it.
  • Unanswered calls end automatically after a short time.

How do I change the video layout on a call?

On a 1-to-1 video call you can choose who fills the screen and pop the call out of the app.

  1. Tap the small self-view (your own camera) to swap it with the other person — whoever you tapped fills the screen.
  2. Tap it again to swap back.
  3. Your self-view is shown large enough to see yourself clearly while you talk.

Minimise a video call and it keeps playing in a small floating window (picture-in-picture) so you can use other apps — tap the window to return to the full call.

What happens if another call comes in while I'm already on a call?

Sogo has call waiting. A second incoming call rings while you're on the first.

  • Accept the new call — your current call ends and the new one starts.
  • Decline the new call, or just ignore it — your current call keeps going.
Good to know

You're only ever connected to one call at a time.

Status (Updates)

Post and view 24-hour status updates.

How do I post a status?
  1. Open the Updates tab.
  2. Tap My Status (or the + on your status ring).
  3. Choose a text, photo or video status.
  4. For video, trim it to the length you want (up to 30 seconds).
  5. Add a caption if you like, then post — it uploads automatically.
Good to know

Statuses disappear automatically after 24 hours.

Tip

Statuses are protected from screenshots and screen recording by default — you can turn this off when you create a status if you don't need it.

How do I view statuses?
  1. Open the Updates tab and tap a contact's status ring.
  2. Photo statuses advance every few seconds; videos play in full.
  3. Tap the right/left side to go forward or back.
  4. Press and hold to pause.
  5. On your own status, see who viewed it; swipe down to close.

Status ring colours:

  • Green ring — a new, unseen status
  • Grey ring — you've already viewed it
Link to this answer
How do I manage or delete my status?
  1. Open your own status from the Updates tab.
  2. See the view count and who viewed it.
  3. Tap the trash icon to delete a status.
Link to this answer
Can people screenshot my status?

By default, Sogo blocks screenshots and screen recording while someone is viewing your status — their capture comes out blank.

  1. When you create a status, keep Protect from screenshots turned on (it's on by default).
  2. Turn it off for that status if you don't need the protection.
Good to know

Protection applies per status, so you can decide each time you post.

Message Requests & Your NumberNew

Who can message you, and who sees your number.

What is a message request?

When someone you haven't connected with messages you for the first time, it arrives as a request instead of a normal chat. You decide whether to answer.

  1. Open the Messages screen — new requests appear in Requests at the top.
  2. Tap Accept to start chatting, or Ignore.
  3. To block, tap ⋯ on the request and choose Block.
Good to know

Requests only show when you have one waiting. Tap See all if there are more than three.

Link to this answer
Why does my message say "Send request"?

The person hasn't connected with you yet, so your first message is sent as a request. Say hi and who you are — you can chat once they accept.

  • You can send a request if you're in a group together or you have their number saved.
  • You can have one open request to each person.
  • Some people turn requests off — you'll see "This person isn't accepting message requests".
Link to this answer
Who can see my phone number?
  • Someone who already has your number saved in their phone sees it in full.
  • Anyone else sees it hidden, unless you choose to show it.
  • People who only follow you on Discover never see your number.
  1. To choose, open a chat (or Group Info) and tap ⋯ → Chat settings on iPhone, or the Privacy section of the contact profile on Android.
  2. Turn Show my number to everyone on or off.
Good to know

Your number never appears in people search, profiles from Discover, or reel comments.

A number request appears in Requests on your Messages screen: "… asked for your number".

  • Tap Share to show them your number.
  • Tap Decline to keep it hidden — they aren't told the reason.

Coins & Earnings

Buy, spend, earn and cash out.

What are coins?

Coins are the only currency inside Sogo. You buy them, earn them and spend them — all from one balance.

  1. Open the Settings tab and tap Wallet.
  2. Your coin balance is at the top.
  3. Below it you'll see how much of that balance you can cash out.
Good to know

Coins you earned — from gifts, ads and trainings — can be turned into money. Coins you bought are for spending inside the app only.

Good to know

Coins you buy can be spent on anything in Sogo, but they can never be cashed out. Only coins you earn can.

Every coin you earn waits 7 days from the day you earned it, then becomes available to withdraw. It gives us time to catch reversed payments and disputes before money leaves.
