Privacy

Straight answers.

We would rather tell you exactly what is protected than use a word that sounds better than the truth.

Encrypted in transit

Messages and calls are encrypted while they travel between your device and our servers — TLS for data, DTLS-SRTP for call media.

Staff can't read your chats

Our admin tools return message metadata only, never contents. A message can be read only after someone reports it, and every such access is recorded.

History stays on your device

Your chat history lives in the app on your phone. Delete the app or your account and it goes with it.

No ads in your chats

Conversations are not an advertising surface. Promoted posts appear only in the status feed, and they are labelled.

What we don't claim. SogoApp is not end-to-end encrypted. Messages are encrypted in transit and stored on our servers, and a lawful request can compel disclosure. The person you are talking to can still screenshot what you send unless you use view-once media or turn on capture protection. The full detail is in the app under Settings → Encryption.

Business information

Who we are and how this works.

Legal entity
Sogoapp LLC, a limited liability company registered in the United States. SogoApp is our mobile application for iOS and Android.
What we sell
A free messaging, calling and live-streaming app, plus coins — a digital in-app currency used to send gifts to creators and to boost a status post for 24 hours. We sell no physical goods and ship nothing.
How customers are charged
All consumer purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and Google Play through their in-app purchase systems. Payment is taken by Apple or Google under their terms; SogoApp never receives or stores card details.
Refunds & cancellations
Because purchases are made through Apple and Google, refunds are handled under their policies — request one via reportaproblem.apple.com or the Google Play order history. Coins are consumable and non-refundable once spent. There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.
Paying creators
Gifts received become diamonds. A creator requests a withdrawal in the app and we pay it to their bank account in a supported country. Creators receive 80%; SogoApp retains 20%. A minimum balance applies before withdrawal.
