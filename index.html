Privacy

Straight answers about your data.

We would rather tell you exactly what is protected than use a word that sounds better than the truth.

Encrypted in transit

Messages and calls are encrypted while they travel between your device and our servers, using TLS for data and DTLS-SRTP for call media.

Staff can't read your chats

Our admin tools show message metadata only — who and when, never the contents. Reading a message is possible only when someone reports it, and every such access is logged.

Your history stays on your device

Chat history lives in the app on your phone. Delete the app or your account and it goes with it.

No ads inside your chats

Your conversations are not an advertising surface. Promoted posts appear only in the status feed, and they are labelled.

What we don't claim. SogoApp is not end-to-end encrypted. Your messages are encrypted in transit and stored on our servers, and a lawful request can compel us to disclose data. The person you are talking to can also still screenshot what you send them, unless you use view-once media or turn on capture protection. You can read the full detail in the app under Settings → Encryption.
