Community Guidelines

Last updated: 28 October 2026

SogoApp is a place to chat, call, go live and share reels with people you care about. These guidelines apply to everything other people can see: your profile photo, name and username, status, reels, live streams and comments. They are part of our Terms of Service.

1. No nudity or sexual content

Do not post, stream or share:

  • Exposed breasts or nipples, genitals, or buttocks — including when partly covered by hands, emojis, filters or see-through clothing;
  • Sexual acts, simulated sexual acts, or sexual touching;
  • Content made to sexually arouse, including sexual poses, stripping, or offering sexual services;
  • Links or invitations to sexual content elsewhere.

This applies on live streams too: turning the camera on yourself or someone else while undressed breaks these guidelines.

2. Zero tolerance: minors

Any sexual or sexualised content involving a person under 18 leads to an immediate, permanent block without warning, and we report it to the relevant authorities.

3. Respect other people

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate against people because of who they are;
  • No violent or gory content, and nothing that promotes self-harm;
  • No sharing other people’s private photos, videos or personal information without their consent;
  • No spam, scams, impersonation or illegal goods.

4. What happens if you break these guidelines

  • First time: we remove the content and send you a warning in the app.
  • Second time: we block your account.
  • Serious violations (such as anything involving minors, sexual exploitation or threats of violence) lead to an immediate, permanent block.

We find violations through reports from users, automated checks and review by our team. We do not apply these checks to your private one-to-one or group chats.

5. Report and block

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, report it from inside the app using Report on the reel, live, status or profile. You can also block any user. Our team reviews reports and acts on content that breaks the rules.

6. Appeals

If you think we removed your content or blocked your account by mistake, email support@sogoappusa.com within 30 days with your phone number and what happened. We will review it again.
